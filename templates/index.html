🔬 System Methodology & Architecture (Judge Mode • Click to Expand) Multi-Agent Pipeline • Algorithmic Details

Automated Workflow (No 20 Sliders): Officers enter only the disaster type and affected city. Spatial coordinates are normalized, partitioning the area into operational sectors. The system automatically ingests live meteorological and digital elevation features.

Trained ML Regressor: The model dynamically processes environmental stress vectors to produce calibrated flood probabilities bounded [0.01, 0.99].

Transparent Priority Engine: Sectors are scored balancing flood risk, population density, vulnerability, and urgency to ensure high demographic centers and imminent hazard sectors are equitably prioritized.

Discrete Optimization & Human Control: Equipment is optimized guaranteeing $\sum \text{Allocated} \le \text{Available}$ and flags shortages when requirements exceed depot inventory. Crucially, the system requires authorized officer approval before field orders are transmitted.
